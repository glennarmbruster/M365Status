Quick Self-Help Troubleshooting Tips

Try these quick steps if Microsoft 365 looks green above, but an app isn't working for you

1. Try the Browser Version

If desktop Outlook or Teams won't load, open outlook.office.com or teams.microsoft.com in a private/incognito window to bypass stale local app caches.

2. Toggle VPN or Wi-Fi

Corporate VPN tunnels or captive hotel/airport Wi-Fi portals frequently block real-time Teams audio and OneDrive sync. Reconnect your VPN or test on a mobile hotspot.

3. Fully Quit & Restart Teams

Closing the Teams window only minimizes it. Right-click the Teams icon in your taskbar/dock, choose Quit, wait 10 seconds, and reopen.

4. Refresh Your Sign-In Session

If documents say "Sign in required," sign out of any Office app, approve your Authenticator MFA prompt, and sign back in with your work email.
